About

Wedding stationery designed in Waterford

Hi, I’m Evan, the designer behind Paper Tide. I’m based in Waterford and my background is in digital design.

Paper Tide grew out of a simple idea: wedding stationery should feel modern, calm and personal, without being complicated or costing the earth. So instead of starting every design from scratch, I’ve created six collections, Linen, Atlantic, Estate, Meadow, Mist and Olive Grove, and I personalise each one carefully for every couple.

The name comes from home. Waterford sits where the River Suir meets the sea, and the quiet line of the tide runs through everything I make: clean layouts, lots of breathing space and details that are easy to love.

I’d love to hear about your plans. Send me a note and let’s make something lovely together.

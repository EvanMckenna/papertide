FAQ

Wedding stationery questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before ordering your wedding invitations. Can’t see your question? Just ask.

What does semi-custom mean?

You choose one of our ready-designed collections and we personalise it with your names, date, venue and wording. It’s more personal than a template you fill in yourself and more affordable than a fully bespoke design.

How much do your wedding invitations cost?

100 invitations with envelopes are €229 and 50 are €149. Invitations with save the dates (100 each) are €349, and the complete suite is €549. All prices include envelopes and personalisation. See our pricing page for everything included.

Can I change the colours or wording?

Yes. The wording is entirely yours, and we can talk about ink colour and small layout tweaks at proof stage. Each collection keeps its overall look so it stays balanced and beautiful.

How many rounds of proofs do I get?

Up to three rounds of changes are included in every order. Nothing goes to print until you approve your final proof.

Can you include Irish (Gaeilge) wording?

Absolutely. Many couples add a line as Gaeilge, or have fully bilingual invitations. Send us your wording and we’ll set it carefully, and we always recommend having a fluent speaker check it too.

Can you draw our venue?

Yes. A line drawing of your venue can be added to any collection for €75 and used across your whole suite. It’s part of what makes the Estate collection special.

Do you offer digital invitations?

Yes. Our digital invitation with a simple RSVP page is €99. It matches your printed design, so you can mix and match, for example printed invitations for older relatives and digital for everyone else.

Do you deliver outside Waterford?

Yes. We design in Waterford and work with couples all over Ireland. Your printed stationery is delivered to your address.

When should I order?

Get in touch about a month before you want your stationery in the post. Save the dates usually go out 9 to 12 months before the wedding, and invitations around 8 to 12 weeks before.

How many invitations do I need?

Usually one per household or couple, not one per guest, so 100 guests often means around 50 to 60 invitations. Our guide to how many invitations you need for 100 guests explains it in detail.

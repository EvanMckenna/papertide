How many wedding invitations do you need for 100 guests?

By Evan, Paper Tide · 16 November 2026

It’s one of the first questions couples ask when ordering stationery, and the answer usually comes as a pleasant surprise: for 100 guests, you’ll probably need somewhere around 50 to 65 invitations, not 100. Here’s how to work out the right number for every piece in your suite.

Invitations: one per household, not per guest

Invitations are sent to each household or couple, not to each individual guest. A married couple shares one invitation. A family of four shares one. Only guests who live on their own, or adult children living away from home, get their own.

So to count your invitations, go through your guest list and group it by address. A guest list of 100 people is often made up of mostly couples with a sprinkling of singles and families, which is why the total usually lands somewhere between 50 and 65.

A worked example

Imagine a 100-guest list that looks like this:

  • 36 couples: 72 guests, 36 invitations
  • 4 families of four: 16 guests, 4 invitations
  • 12 single guests: 12 guests, 12 invitations

That’s 100 guests but only 52 invitations. Add a few spares (more on that below) and an order of around 60 would cover everything comfortably.

Always order spares

We recommend ordering roughly 10 to 15% more than your count. Spares cover:

  • Last-minute additions to the guest list, or a second round of invitations if some guests decline.
  • Mistakes while addressing envelopes.
  • A few to keep: one for each of you, one for each set of parents, and one for your photographer to style on the day.

Reprinting a small number later almost always costs more per card than adding them to your first order, so it’s worth building in a buffer.

Save the dates

Save the dates follow the same rule as invitations: one per household. Send them only to people you’re sure will be invited. Most couples order the same quantity as their invitations.

Menus

Menus are counted at the table, so it depends on your style:

  • One per guest: placed on each setting. For 100 guests, order 100 plus a few spares.
  • One per couple: shared between two places, around 50.
  • One or two per table: for 10 tables of 10, that’s 10 to 20.

Confirm your numbers once RSVPs are in, as menus and place names should be the last pieces you print.

Place names

Place names are the one piece you’ll need for every single guest, including children who have their own seat. For 100 guests, that’s 100 place names, plus a couple of spares for surprise changes on the day.

Thank-you cards

Thank-you cards are usually sent per household or per gift, so the number is close to your invitation count. Remember to add any suppliers, readers, musicians or family helpers you’d like to thank personally.

Quick reference for 100 guests

  • Invitations: about 50 to 65, including spares
  • Save the dates: the same as invitations
  • Menus: 100 (one per guest), about 50 (one per couple) or 10 to 20 (per table)
  • Place names: 100, plus a few spares
  • Thank-you cards: about 50 to 65, plus suppliers and helpers

How it works

How semi-custom wedding invitations work

Semi-custom means the design work is already done beautifully, and we tailor it to you. You get stationery that feels personal, without the price or the wait of a fully bespoke design.

  1. 01

    1. Choose your collection and pieces

    Start by browsing Linen, Atlantic, Estate, Meadow, Mist and Olive Grove. Then pick what you need: maybe just invitations, invitations with save the dates, or the complete suite with menus, place names and thank-you cards.

  2. 02

    2. Send us your details

    Use our enquiry form to tell us your names, wedding date, venue, guest numbers and the collection you love. If you’re stuck on the words, our guide to wedding invitation wording in Ireland has plenty of examples.

  3. 03

    3. Review your proof

    We set your wording in your chosen design and send you a digital proof. Want a different line break, an Irish phrase or your venue drawn in? Just say. Up to three rounds of changes are included, and nothing is printed until you’re completely happy.

  4. 04

    4. Printed and delivered

    Once you approve your proof, your stationery is professionally printed on heavyweight card with matching envelopes and delivered to you, ready to address and post.

When should we start?

Ideally, get in touch around a month before you want your save the dates or invitations in the post. That leaves plenty of time for proofs and printing without any rush. Our guide on when to send wedding invitations in Ireland covers the full timeline.

Ready to start?

Pick a collection and send your details. We’ll reply within a day with your first proof plan.

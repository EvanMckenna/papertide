Questions

All FAQs

What does semi-custom mean?

You choose one of our ready-made designs and we personalise it with your names, date, venue and wording. You see a proof before anything is printed.

When should I order?

Get in touch about a month before you want your stationery in the post. Save the dates usually go out 9 to 12 months before, invitations 8 to 12 weeks before.

Can I change the wording or colours?

Yes. The wording is entirely yours, and small tweaks to ink colour and layout are fine at proof stage. Up to three rounds of changes are included.

Do prices include envelopes?

Yes. Every package includes envelopes, personalisation and up to three proof rounds. Delivery anywhere in Ireland.
